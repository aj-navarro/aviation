*FLUTTER*

In aviation, the English term **"flutter"** refers to an aeroelastic vibration phenomenon. In Spanish, it is most commonly translated as:

- **"flutter"** (the English term is often kept as-is in technical Spanish)
- **"aleteo"** (literal translation, sometimes used)
- **"vibración aeroelástica"** (descriptive term)
- **"galope"** — no, that's a different phenomenon (galloping)

The most standard term in Spanish aviation/aeronautical engineering texts is **"flutter"** (borrowed directly), sometimes written in italics. **"Aleteo"** is also used but can be ambiguous.

Strictly preferred Spanish term: **flutter** (anglicism) or **vibración autoinducida por aeroelasticidad**.

*DIVE SPEED*

In aviation, **"dive speed"** in Spanish is most commonly:

- **"velocidad de picado"** — the standard aviation term (a *picado* is a dive)

Related terms you may also encounter:

- **"velocidad límite de picado"** — dive limit speed
- **"VD"** — design dive speed (the symbol used in certification, e.g., CS-23/FAR-23)
- **"velocidad de descenso"** — descent speed (not the same; a descent is not a dive)

If you mean the certification term **"design dive speed"**, that is **"velocidad de picado de diseño"** (symbol **VD**).

So the direct translation is **"velocidad de picado."**
